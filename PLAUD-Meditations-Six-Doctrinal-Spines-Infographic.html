What the lattice reveals

Three structural findings.

V ↔ VIII

The hub-and-street pattern.

Between them, Tablets V and VIII hold 55% of the corpus. The largest cross-tablet flow (202 refs) runs between them: the received voice heard in secret is the sacred-ordinary walked in the marketplace. Not two doctrines. One breath.

61 hubs

The deep-integration nodes.

61 meditations carry 15+ memo cross-references — the corpus's high-degree nodes. Entering through any of them puts a reader one hop away from the whole lattice. The Aug 17 · 19:05 seam alone is referenced across every tablet.

Feb → Sep

The seed-to-arrival arc.

Every August tablet has a Feb or March root file that named its doctrine before it walked out. Every September file has an August tablet it walks out from. The archive reads chronologically as one continuous covenantal formation.
