    How to read this map

    Every node is a record — sized by how structurally central it is to the whole corpus (how often the shortest path between two other records passes through it), colored by which community of closely-related records it belongs to. Every edge is a real, hand-verified connection; brighter, thicker edges are structural bridges between otherwise-distant parts of the corpus. Nothing here is a ranking of spiritual importance — it is a map of how the record cites and answers itself.
