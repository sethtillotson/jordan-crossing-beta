The four movements

Mystery Mode is an ordered way of entering, listening, testing, and responding. Not liturgy as a replacement for gathered worship — but a threshold with shape.

Name

You name what you are carrying. The site does not require a confession — only a doorway.

Receive

The site presents one original record. Not five hundred. Not a wall of links. One. With its title, date, content, Scripture references, and source information.

Discern

What did you actually encounter? The site does not announce its interpretation. It asks — and offers navigation, not psychological profiling.

Return

Every session eventually releases you back into life. You may continue, return later, read the Scripture, or leave and carry the question honestly.
