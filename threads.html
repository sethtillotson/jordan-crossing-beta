Editorial Connection

This beta does not claim to map the whole archive. Following a corpus-wide truth-correction, it now shows a reviewed constellation built from each meditation's own verified Cross-Reference Appendix — 404 records (of 409 local total) cross-referenced by 3,270 real, hand-verified links, each tied to its correct Stone Tablet window (the specific volume in the author's Living Topology of Transformation series it belongs to by date) rather than an inferred doctrinal label. The remaining ~5 meditations mirrored into this beta are fully readable at their own page (browse them from the Archive), but are not yet cross-referenced, so they are not shown as part of this reviewed constellation.

How to read a connection

author-confirmed

The connection is explicitly present in the working record or confirmed by the author.

editorial connection

A human-reviewed bridge offered for consideration, not presented as revelation.

open

A question or possible relationship not yet settled.
