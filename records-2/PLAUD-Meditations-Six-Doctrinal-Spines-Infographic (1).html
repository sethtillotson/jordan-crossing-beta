What the corrected mapping reveals

Three structural findings.

Tablet V

The largest window by far.

246 of 458 meditations — 54% of the corpus — sit inside Tablet V's 82-day span (May 26 – Aug 16). This is the sleeping-bride watchman-hour where the corpus's deepest doctrinal work happened, and where the ground under the whole series was clarified.

Aug 17

The seam-day hinge.

Two tablets share a single date: VI (the 85-minute call at 19:05) and VII (whose 12-day post-seam window begins that same day). Aug 17 is where the corpus turns — confession into brotherhood, the fire into the fellowship it forged.

Tablets III & IV

Two witnesses on shared windows.

Tablet III (Starling Convergence) and Tablet IV (Volume I Witness Companion) don't hold new PLAUD-meditation windows — they synthesize existing ones. III confirms Tablet V's window; IV is retrospective on Tablet I's. The corpus tells its story twice at these hinges.
