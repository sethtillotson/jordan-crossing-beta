Prophetic Word Seth Tillotson Thursday, August 13, 2026 12:33 PM On this day I was praying in the Spirit and I asked the Lord, "The bride of Christ has fallen asleep. How can we be so deceived?" And the Lord said, "Oh, see how far you’ve come and how far you have fallen. Come out of her, My children; Wake up, My Church. For My Spirit is in your midst, and I am coming quickly. Why have you rejected Me? It is a sinful generation that seeks a sign; But I say to you, none will be given them. Believe My words. Return to Me. Why do you compare yourselves to yourselves? Did the depth of the treasures of My glory not shine so on Peter that even his shadow healed all whom it touched? And yet you seek signs and wonders, but do not believe My words. Repent and return to the works you did at first. Now take up your cross and follow Me. For I have sent you a helper, My Holy Spirit, to teach you all things and guide you in all things. Remember Me; For whoever has, more will be given him, But for those who do not have Me, I am coming to take what little he has. And for all who hear My call and return to Me, You will receive the promises of My Glory I have not yet extinguished the flame of your lamp stand, For My lovingkindness and longsuffering endures until I come again." Maranatha. Come, Lord Jesus. ---
