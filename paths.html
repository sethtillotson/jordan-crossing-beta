Editorial Connection · Sourced, Not Invented

The paths below are copied from the corpus-map documents Seth keeps in the Second Brain. Following the Phase 11 full corpus rebuild (September 2026), all 456 meditations now have a local page in this beta, fully reviewed and cross-referenced against a corpus-wide truth correction — see Archive for the full browsable list. 35 of these 48 curated path steps now link to a local page, confirmed by matching each step's own label against that record's real, content-derived title on its exact recorded day — not by filename, which turned out to be unreliable (some source filenames in the wider corpus do not match their own recorded content, a genuine data-quality issue in the underlying export). The remaining 13 steps still link to the Second Brain because no local record could be confidently confirmed as the same meditation. Steps already confirmed are marked in this beta; the rest are marked external source.
