What "reviewed" and "mirrored" mean here

reviewed records have passed through one of the Cross-Reference passes: they carry labeled thread connections and appear in Threads. mirrored records are just as fully readable — the complete original text, the same page — but have not yet been cross-referenced, so no thread claims are made for them. Both kinds are local pages in this app; neither depends on the author's private drive to open. The corpus's own working record documents 451 meditations in total; this repository currently mirrors 410 of the underlying files, of which a handful are supporting documents rather than meditations proper — see the by-month counts below for exactly what is and isn't present yet.

See the shape of the lattice

The corpus's own Cross-Reference project is now complete: every real meditation carries a verified appendix of cross-references, organized around six governing doctrinal spines (the August "Tablets" each record feeds into or walks out from). Two visual companions to this archive: The Six Doctrinal Spines (what each spine is, and how files flow between them) and Timeline of Six Spines (their weekly growth across all seven months of the record, February through September 2026).

Where this came from

The reviewed records are drawn from a Corpus Map and Cross-Reference passes the author keeps alongside the source meditations. The mirrored records are the same raw meditation files, rendered the same way, without those pass-level connections claimed yet. As more meditations are reviewed and cross-referenced, they move from "mirrored" to "reviewed" and gain entries in Threads and Corpus Paths — the underlying page does not change, only its labeled connections.
