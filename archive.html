See the shape of the lattice

The corpus's own Cross-Reference project is now complete: every real meditation carries a verified appendix of cross-references, organized around the eight true Stone Tablets — the specific written volumes in the author's Living Topology of Transformation series, each covering a specific window of days, that a meditation belongs to by date (not an inferred doctrinal category). Visual companions to this archive: Read the Eight Stone Tablets (the volumes themselves), The Eight Stone Tablets Infographic (what each tablet is, and how files flow between them), Timeline of the Eight Stone Tablets (their weekly growth across all eight months of the record, February through September 2026), and Corpus Architecture.

Where this came from

The reviewed records are drawn from each meditation's own Cross-Reference Appendix — added directly to the file, hand-verified by the author against the eight Stone Tablet volumes themselves. (An earlier version of this feature parsed summary "pass" ledger documents instead of each file's own appendix, and some of what it inferred did not hold up under that verification; the entire cross-reference layer was rebuilt from the corrected, per-file source once the author caught and fixed it, then rebuilt again from a further-verified corpus in Phase 11 — see CHANGELOG.md for the full account.)
