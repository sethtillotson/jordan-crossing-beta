Why this page shows counts, not content

The seven records this beta has fully built — reviewed, formatted, and threaded — remain the only individual meditations this site presents in full. The wider corpus behind them is real, dated, and much larger, but it has not yet passed the same review this project's own governing document requires before publication: private people and protected internal arcs remain private, and the corpus is introduced “in reviewed layers,” not as a bulk export. Until each layer is reviewed, this page shows only what can be said safely — how much exists, and when it was recorded.
